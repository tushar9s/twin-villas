Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Twin Villas Hospitality Guidelines

Terms & Conditions Privacy Policy Cookie Policy

At Twin Villas, accessible via our website and official booking platforms, we prioritize your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the measures we undertake to safeguard your personal data.

Privacy Promise: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party telemarketers or ad brokers. Your data is strictly used for reservation logistics, guest verification, and hospitality coordination.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, make an inquiry, or book a stay at Twin Villas, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, telephone number, WhatsApp contact number, and email address.
  • Reservation Details: Chosen villa (e.g., Twins Villa 30, Twins Villa 93, Twins Villa 94, Twins Villa 95, Twins Villa 119, or White House), check-in and check-out dates, number of adults and children, and special hospitality requests.
  • Verification Documents: Government photo ID cards (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving Licence) required at check-in for mandatory state compliance and police guest records.
  • Payment & Transaction Info: Transaction reference IDs and payment receipt confirmations. We do not store credit card numbers or banking passwords on our servers.
  • Browsing Data: Anonymous technical information such as browser type, referring pages, IP address, and time spent on our website to optimize platform speed and mobile responsiveness.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate hospitality and business operations:

  • Reservation Processing: Generating official booking vouchers, payment confirmations, and invoice receipts.
  • Pre-Arrival Logistics: Sending turn-by-turn Google Maps driving directions, caretaker contact details, and key handover instructions.
  • On-Site Hospitality: Coordinating with our resident caretakers to prepare your villa, arrange extra bedding, and cater to requested amenities.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Adhering to Maharashtra state guest verification and local police reporting mandates.
  • Guest Support: Responding promptly to your messages on WhatsApp, phone calls, or emails regarding date changes or local sightseeing.

3. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We value your trust and do not monetize your data:

  • No Commercial Sale of Data: We will never sell, rent, or lease your personal information to marketing firms or third-party advertisers.
  • Service Providers: Information is shared only with verified service personnel (such as our on-site villa caretaker) strictly necessary for your stay.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose personal data if required by law, court order, or formal request from law enforcement agencies for safety and security purposes.

4. WhatsApp & Direct Communication

For your convenience, our website integrates direct click-to-chat links with our reservation desk via WhatsApp. Communications, guest counts, and vouchers exchanged through WhatsApp are governed by WhatsApp's privacy policies and end-to-end encryption standards.

5. Data Retention & Security

  • We implement rigorous physical, technical, and organizational security protocols to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
  • Guest identity records required by local authorities are archived securely for the period mandated by law and subsequently disposed of safely.

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your personal data:

  • You may request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • You may request corrections to any inaccurate contact information.
  • You may request that we delete your contact information from our customer records once all legal compliance periods have passed.
