Welcome to Twin Villas. By booking and staying at any of our properties (Twins Villa 30, Twins Villa 93, Twins Villa 94, Twins Villa 95, Twins Villa 119, or Twins Villa White House), you agree to comply with and be bound by the following Terms and Conditions, house rules, and community guidelines.

Quick Summary: Valid government ID is mandatory for all guests at check-in. Standard check-in is 1:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Quiet hours are observed outdoors from 10:00 PM onwards to respect society regulations.

1. Booking Confirmation & Payments

A reservation is confirmed only upon receipt of the advance booking deposit through our official payment methods (Direct Bank Transfer, UPI, or sanctioned reservation channels).

  • The remaining balance of the booking amount must be paid either prior to arrival or at the time of check-in before the keys and villa access are handed over.
  • Tariffs quoted are exclusive of optional personalized chef or meal packages unless explicitly stated in your booking confirmation voucher.
  • Rates are subject to seasonal revisions, weekend surcharges, and festive peak dates. Confirmed bookings with received deposits will not be subject to subsequent tariff increases.

2. Check-In & Check-Out Schedule

  • Standard Check-In Time: 1:00 PM
  • Standard Check-Out Time: 11:00 AM
  • Early Check-in / Late Check-out: Permitted solely upon prior written confirmation and subject to villa availability and scheduled cleaning turnaround. Additional hourly charges may apply.

3. Government ID Verification & Guest Registry

In strict compliance with Maharashtra state tourism and local police reporting norms:

  • Every adult guest (18+ years) must present an original, valid Government-issued photo identification proof (Aadhaar Card, Driving Licence, Passport, or Voter ID) upon arrival. PAN cards are not recognized as valid address proof.
  • Foreign nationals must present a valid passport and Indian entry visa.
  • Only guests declared and registered during booking are permitted to stay overnight on the premises. Unregistered visitors or extra overnight guests will incur standard additional occupancy fees.

4. Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit is collected upon check-in to cover any potential damages, missing items, or excessive post-stay cleaning requirements.

  • The property, furniture, electronics, and swimming pool area will be inspected prior to guest departure.
  • The security deposit is refunded promptly at check-out via UPI or cash, provided no damages or infractions have occurred.
  • In case damages exceed the security deposit amount, the primary guest who completed the booking assumes full legal and financial liability to cover the remaining repair or replacement costs.

5. Private Swimming Pool Safety Guidelines

Each villa features a private swimming pool or waterfall plunge pool for your exclusive enjoyment:

  • No Lifeguard on Duty: Guests swim entirely at their own risk.
  • Child Supervision: Children under the age of 14 must be accompanied and supervised by a responsible adult at all times within the pool area.
  • No Glassware: Glass bottles, glass tumblers, and sharp crockery are strictly prohibited in and immediately around the pool deck to prevent hazardous breakage.
  • Swimwear: Clean nylon or synthetic swimming attire is required. Cotton clothing can clog pool filtration systems.
  • Diving Prohibition: Diving, flips, and reckless stunts into plunge pools are strictly prohibited due to shallow water depths.

6. Noise Levels, Music & Society Etiquette

Twin Villas is situated within a serene, secure gated community:

  • Outdoor Music Curfew: In accordance with local society regulations and environmental noise control laws, outdoor speakers and poolside music must be turned off completely by 10:00 PM.
  • Indoor music is permitted throughout the night inside the closed air-conditioned living halls, provided volume levels do not disturb neighboring properties.
  • Unruly behavior, excessive shouting, or disorderly conduct that infringes on community peace may result in forfeiture of security deposit and immediate cancellation of the stay without refund.

7. Prohibited Items & Strict House Rules

  • Smoking Policy: Smoking is strictly forbidden inside all air-conditioned bedrooms and enclosed suites. Guests may smoke responsibly in open outdoor areas, garden courtyards, and balconies. Please dispose of cigarette butts in provided ashtrays.
  • Illegal Substances: Possession, distribution, or consumption of narcotics, drugs, and illegal substances is strictly banned. Violations will be reported immediately to law enforcement authorities.
  • Hazardous Materials: Firearms, fireworks, explosives, and combustible chemicals are strictly banned from the property.
  • Commercial Photography: Professional commercial shoots, movie productions, and corporate events require prior written consent and distinct commercial permits.

8. Caretaker Hospitality & Housekeeping

  • A dedicated resident caretaker is available on-site to assist with check-in, key handover, daily trash disposal, basic tea/coffee assistance, and emergency support.
  • Caretakers work on reasonable shifts (typically 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM). Please treat on-site staff with dignity and courtesy.

9. Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

  • More than 7 Days Prior to Check-in: Eligible for date rescheduling within 60 days (subject to villa availability and seasonal rate adjustments) or partial refund as specified in your booking voucher.
  • Within 7 Days of Check-in: Advance deposit is non-refundable as the villa has been reserved exclusively for your dates and denied to other inquiries.
  • No-Shows / Early Departures: No refunds or credits are issued for guest no-shows, delayed arrivals, or voluntary early check-outs.

10. Force Majeure & Limitation of Liability

All villas are equipped with 100% generator power backup and high-speed optical Wi-Fi. However, Twin Villas management shall not be held liable for temporary disruptions caused by external regional grid failures, severe weather, natural calamities, highway blockades, or government-imposed restrictions.
