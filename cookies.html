Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Twin Villas Hospitality Guidelines

Terms & Conditions Privacy Policy Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how Twin Villas utilizes them on our website, and how you can manage your cookie preferences to ensure a smooth, secure browsing experience.

What Are Cookies? Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you navigating smoothly, and enhance user experience.

1. Types of Cookies We Use

Our website utilizes a minimal, privacy-conscious set of cookies categorized as follows:

  • Essential & Technical Cookies:
    These cookies are strictly necessary for the core operation of our website. They enable basic functionalities such as fast page rendering, secure navigation across villa pages, and mobile responsiveness.
  • Preference & Functionality Cookies:
    These allow the website to remember choices you make while browsing — such as recently selected villa gallery views, filter categories on our Amenities bento grid, or audio/video playback preferences.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies:
    We may use privacy-compliant, aggregated web analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., which villa photos are viewed most, average session duration, and device screen sizes). This data is completely anonymous and never linked to your personal identity.
  • Embedded Third-Party Cookies:
    Certain interactive elements on our website are powered by trusted third-party providers:
    • Google Maps: Powers our interactive location map on Khopoli-Pali Road to provide real-time turn-by-turn navigation.
    • Google Web Fonts & FontAwesome: Cached stylesheet resources to display typography and vector icons quickly.
    • WhatsApp Click-to-Chat: Streamlines communication directly to our reservation desk.

2. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You have full authority to accept, decline, or delete cookies at any time via your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain essential cookies may affect the interactive map or multimedia galleries.

Here is how you can manage cookies across popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data / Block all cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.
  • Mobile Devices (Android / iOS): In your phone's browser app settings under Privacy / Site Settings.

3. Policy Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect technological improvements, security patches, or updated legal guidelines. Any changes will become effective immediately upon posting the updated policy on this page with an updated revision date.
